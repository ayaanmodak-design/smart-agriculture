Irrigation Workflow

  1. Sensor collects soil moisture data
  2. Data is sent to cloud server
  3. AI model predicts irrigation need
  4. Advisory is sent to farmer
  5. Farmer irrigates the field

Sensors Used

  • Soil Moisture Sensor
  • Temperature Sensor
  • Humidity Sensor
  • Rainfall Sensor

Crop Stages

Germination
Seed starts sprouting, needs low water
Tillering
Side shoots develop, water need increases
Grand Growth
Fast growth stage, highest water requirement
Maturity
Water requirement reduces, crop is ready for harvest
